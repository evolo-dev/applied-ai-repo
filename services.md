# Our Services

## RAG & AI Agents

Transform enterprise knowledge (PDFs, databases, internal systems) into actionable AI agents.

**Use cases:**
- Internal knowledge bases → searchable agents
- Document repositories → Q&A bots
- Data warehouses → natural language querying

**Technologies we use:**
- Vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, pgvector)
- RAG frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex)
- Agent orchestration (Dockerflow, AutoGen)

**Typical outcomes:**
- 10-50x reduction in search/knowledge lookup time
- Human-in-the-loop validation for accuracy-critical use cases
- Audit trails for compliance requirements
